# First Vertical Slice — v0.1

## Question

Can several participants remain inside one evolving encounter without a human manually carrying the living context between isolated chats—while every voice remains recoverable from the chord?

The slice does not pass merely because deterministic replay works. It passes only if continuity survives interruption.

## Architectural purpose

- **Project0 enables co-character:** form together.
- **TranchNode/Witness enables co-inheritance:** carry together.
- **Band Runtime enables co-faithfulness:** remain together.

The slice must prove the third responsibility without weakening the first two.

## Room

One local browser room contains:

- one human Formation Trace channel;
- three agent-addressable channels (test clients allowed);
- one Corpus OS / Autodiscography retrieval-resonance channel;
- timeline clips for text, audio, code, traces, observations, and proposals;
- shared causal transport;
- `rings | nearby | projection | no` recognition receipts;
- declared projection controls, including foreignness and protected silence;
- a deterministic WAV mix and exportable receipt bundle.

The browser room is a PA, not authority. The shared event field is canonical; every projection is disposable and declared.

## Minimum event vocabulary

`session.opened` · `participant.joined` · `clip.proposed` · `clip.admitted` · `clip.rejected` · `recognition.recorded` · `anticipation.proposed` · `anticipation.contested` · `projection.policy_declared` · `mix.rendered` · `session.closed`

The accepted event sequence is authoritative. Administrative state is computed by projection.

## Scripted interruption encounter

1. Open a session and admit one human Formation Trace.
2. Let three agent clients and one corpus client inspect the same bounded projection.
3. Deposit separable proposal clips.
4. Render candidates with and without a dominant historical contour.
5. Record at least one `rings`, one `nearby`, one `projection`, and one `no`.
6. Persist and close the session; stop the runtime process.
7. Restart from the event log and content-addressed artifacts.
8. Replay immediately before and after one recognition event; verify the earlier cut is byte-identical.
9. Contest that recognition by appending a new event; verify the original receipt remains queryable.
10. Mute one channel in a new declared projection; recover its original stem unchanged.
11. Export the receipt bundle at two causal cuts.
12. From the later bundle alone, reconstruct who intervened, what was carried forward, which projection policy changed, and what remains unresolved or withheld.

## Acceptance assertions

The slice succeeds only when all are true:

- interruption and restart do not alter accepted event ordering, artifact identity, or prior projection bytes;
- a returning participant can re-enter the same unresolved thread without a human-authored summary becoming authority;
- recognition changes permitted future projection behavior only after its accepted sequence;
- all mix contributions remain recoverable by participant and channel with disclosure intact;
- the export bundle proves how continuity was established rather than merely proving that events occurred;
- the resulting chord never prevents recovery of its voices.

A tape recorder passes **Record**. This slice must pass **Remain**.

## Adversarial gates

1. **Past remains past** — later recognition cannot change replay at an earlier causal cut.
2. **Anticipation cannot self-confirm** — anticipated material cannot raise its own weight without attributable human recognition.
3. **Re-wetting appends** — contestation creates a new event and preserves the prior receipt.
4. **Sovereign stem recovery** — every included contribution is recoverable with original bytes and disclosure.
5. **Mute is not deletion** — muting affects only one declared projection.
6. **Refusal is not negative consent** — rejection cannot authorize training, disclosure, or canonization.
7. **Foreignness is genuinely foreign** — outside or unexplored material remains provenance-distinct.
8. **Protected silence** — anticipation cannot be emitted inside a protected interval.
9. **Counterfactual visibility** — recognition records which alternatives were actually presented.
10. **Concurrent agents** — same-position proposals remain separate and receive deterministic accepted ordering.
11. **Projection honesty** — causal cut, policy, and disclosure are mandatory; omission fails closed.
12. **Disclosure survives mixdown** — unauthorized stems are refused or redacted without falsely claiming completeness.
13. **Continuity survives interruption** — shutdown and restart preserve enough addressed structure to reconstruct carried influence, interventions, unresolved remainder, and lawful future permissions.

## Scope filter

> Does this help participants remain together, or merely help produce an artifact alone?

Cut or defer features that answer primarily with the second:

- automatic recognition;
- self-hardening anticipation;
- output-quality optimizers that obscure stems or provenance;
- canonical-feeling master surfaces;
- provider-dependent replay;
- summaries that replace rather than cite the causal field;
- convenience mutation of projections.

## Explicit non-goals

No full DAW, low-latency performance engine, autonomous conductor, cloud tenancy, fine-tuning, emotion inference, economic layer, or corpus-wide interpretation.
