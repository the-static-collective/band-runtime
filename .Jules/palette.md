## 2024-08-04 - Designing for Non-Authoritative Collaboration

**Learning:** In highly collaborative, human-and-agent runtime environments (like Band Runtime), UX must actively preserve foreignness and provenance. Standard UX patterns (like "merging" to a master truth or hiding AI inference as plain text) violate the core law that "an emergent mix never becomes canonical authority." Rejection is just as important as acceptance and must remain distinct and addressable.
**Action:** When designing state transitions or presentation layers for shared encounters, explicitly expose the causal history, separate proposals from accepted historical facts, and ensure users can interrupt and return to specific causal cuts without altering the immutable event strata.
